# Business Analytics for E-Commerce in Construction Materials
The project is based on my real experience in handling business analytics for M/S L1 Supply Networks Ltd, a Chennai-based start-up which aimed to bring e-commerce to construction materials. My tasks involved CRM analytics, account flows, employee productivity analytics, digital marketing leads analytics, and fulfilment metrics. A few snippets have been recreated here.

## Project Synopsis
The project aims to recreate the following reports using synthetic data based on real world constraints and implications:  
1. Enquiry Analytics
2. Employee Productivity Analytics
3. Fulfilment Analytics
  
The reports will reveal a snapshot of analytics as per Indian Fiscal Year 2022-23 (FY23 - April 1, 2022 to March 31, 2023).  

## Background and Context
* The e-commerce business acts as a middleman between customer (construction project, B2B/B2C) and vendor (manufactures construction product)
* The firm negotiates for best rates separately with both sides ultimately coming to an agreement, and the difference in customer price and vendor price is taken as the firm's margin
* Products under 4 categories are dealt with namely Cement, AAC Blocks, Steel and Concrete for Chennai region
* There is no separate inventory to store products, and fulfilment is handled by third parties as per best rates
* The employees are classified into three teams namely Sales (10 people), Procurement (8 people) and Fulfilment (5 people)
* Sales team faces the customers, Procurement team faces the vendors, and the Fulfilment team handles logistics
* For FY23, it has been assumed that the firm has 500 customers
* The business is assumed to have 3 cement vendors, 4 AAC blocks vendors, 4 steel vendors, and 3 concrete vendors
* Transportation and logistics shall be handled by 10 parties
